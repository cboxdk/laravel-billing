# Cbox Billing v0.11.2

Cbox Billing v0.11.2 adds a percentage price model: a rate of a reported value, such as 1.5% of the transaction volume a meter sums. A percentage price can add a fixed fee per event or per unit, a minimum and a maximum per period, and tiers of rate by volume. Every change is additive, so this is a patch inside the 0.11 line. It does not depend on v0.11.1 and can ship with it or after it.

## Added

- `PricingModel::Percentage`, with `isPercentage()`. `isTiered()` is `false` for it.
- `Cbox\Billing\Catalog\ValueObjects\PercentageRate`: a percentage held exactly as parts per million. `ofPercent('1.5')` reads a decimal string with at most four decimals, from 0 to 100. `ofPartsPerMillion()` and `toPercentString()` convert both ways. A rate never passes through a float.
- `PercentageTier { ?int upTo, PercentageRate rate }`: a bracket by volume, in the price currency's minor units, the last one unbounded.
- `PercentageTierMode`: `Graduated` charges each slice of volume at its own tier's rate, and `Volume` charges all of it at the tier the total lands in.
- `PercentagePrice`: the terms. It holds a currency, the tiers, the tier mode, and an optional `fixedFee`, `minimum` and `maximum`. It is checked when it is built and throws `MalformedPercentagePrice` for no tiers, tiers out of order, an amount in another currency or below zero, or a minimum above the maximum.
- `Cbox\Billing\Catalog\Pricing\PercentageCalculator::charge(PercentagePrice, int $volume, int $feeUnits = 0): PercentageCharge`. The charge carries the volume, the fee units, the fees and the total, and `bound` says when the minimum or maximum set the total.
- `Price` gains an optional `percentage` constructor argument and a `percentageTerms()` accessor. `Price::amountFor($volume)` charges a percentage price with no fee units, and throws `MalformedPercentagePrice` when the terms are missing or in another currency than the price.

## Rounding

Every term is summed exactly and the total is rounded once, half up, to a whole minor unit. Nothing is rounded per event or per slice. The minimum and maximum then bound the rounded total. A period with no volume and no fee units is charged nothing, minimum included.

| Case | Result |
| --- | --- |
| 1.5% of 123457 DKK minor | `1852` (18.51855 rounds half up) |
| 1.5% of 99 DKK minor, three events | `1` (one rounding of 1.485, not three of 0.495) |
| 2.9% of 100000 + 25 × 180 | `7400` |
| 1% of 100000, minimum 5000 | `5000`, bound `minimum` |

## Upgrading

Nothing to do for existing prices. Code with a `match` over `PricingModel` that has no `default` arm should handle `Percentage`. Code that offers every `PricingModel::cases()` as a choice for a recurring price should leave `Percentage` out: it prices a reported value, not a quantity of seats or units.
