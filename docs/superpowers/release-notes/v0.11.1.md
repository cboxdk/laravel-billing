# Cbox Billing v0.11.1

Cbox Billing v0.11.1 adds two things:

- A billing cycle can run every N days, weeks, months or years, so a price can bill weekly, every two weeks, quarterly or semi-annually. Monthly and yearly cycles behave exactly as before.
- A percentage price model: a rate of a reported value, such as 1.5% of the transaction volume a meter sums.

Every change is additive, so this is a patch inside the 0.11 line.

## Added: billing cycles of any length

- `BillingInterval::Weekly` and `BillingInterval::Daily`, with `isDayBased()` and `days()`. `months()` throws a `LogicException` for a day-based unit, and `days()` throws one for a month-based unit.
- `Cbox\Billing\Subscription\ValueObjects\BillingFrequency`: a unit and a count, such as `quarterly()` (3 months), `semiAnnually()` (6 months) or `of(BillingInterval::Weekly, 2)`. Equal spans fold onto one value, so 12 months is a year and 7 days is a week. `key()` gives the durable key (`month`, `year`, `week`, `day`, or `{count}-{unit}` such as `3-month`). `fromKey()` and `tryFromKey()` read the key and the common spellings (`monthly`, `quarterly`, `semi_annual` and so on). It also offers `months()`, `days()`, `cadence()`, `monthlyShare()` (an exact fraction for normalising revenue), `compare()` and `equals()`. A cycle spans at most three years.
- `BillingCycle` gains two optional constructor arguments, `intervalCount` (default 1) and `anchorYear` (default null), and a `frequency()` accessor. `anchoredOnSignup()`, `calendarFirst()` and `forAnchor()` accept either a `BillingInterval` or a `BillingFrequency`.
- Multi-month cycles keep the month-end clamp: a Jan 31 quarterly signup bills Jan 31, Apr 30, Jul 31. Week- and day-based cycles count whole local days from the anchor date, so a boundary stays on local midnight across a daylight-saving change.
- A cycle whose phase the anchor month cannot fix (day-based, every five months, every two years) needs `anchorYear`. The constructor throws `InvalidArgumentException` without it. The factories set it for you.
- `calendarFirst()` pins quarterly cycles to calendar quarters and weekly cycles to Monday.

## Upgrading: billing cycles

Nothing to do for monthly and yearly cycles. Code with a `match` over `BillingInterval` that has no `default` arm should handle `Weekly` and `Daily` before it creates cycles on them.

## Added: percentage prices

- `PricingModel::Percentage`, with `isPercentage()`. `isTiered()` is `false` for it.
- `Cbox\Billing\Catalog\ValueObjects\PercentageRate`: a percentage held exactly as parts per million. `ofPercent('1.5')` reads a decimal string with at most four decimals, from 0 to 100. `ofPartsPerMillion()` and `toPercentString()` convert both ways. A rate never passes through a float.
- `PercentageTier { ?int upTo, PercentageRate rate }`: a bracket by volume, in the price currency's minor units, the last one unbounded.
- `PercentageTierMode`: `Graduated` charges each slice of volume at its own tier's rate, and `Volume` charges all of it at the tier the total lands in.
- `PercentagePrice`: the terms. It holds a currency, the tiers, the tier mode, and an optional `fixedFee`, `minimum` and `maximum`. It is checked when it is built and throws `MalformedPercentagePrice` for no tiers, tiers out of order, an amount in another currency or below zero, or a minimum above the maximum.
- `Cbox\Billing\Catalog\Pricing\PercentageCalculator::charge(PercentagePrice, int $volume, int $feeUnits = 0): PercentageCharge`. The charge carries the volume, the fee units, the fees and the total, and `bound` says when the minimum or maximum set the total.
- `Price` gains an optional `percentage` constructor argument and a `percentageTerms()` accessor. `Price::amountFor($volume)` charges a percentage price with no fee units, and throws `MalformedPercentagePrice` when the terms are missing or in another currency than the price.

## Percentage rounding

Every term is summed exactly and the total is rounded once, half up, to a whole minor unit. Nothing is rounded per event or per slice. The minimum and maximum then bound the rounded total. A period with no volume and no fee units is charged nothing, minimum included.

| Case | Result |
| --- | --- |
| 1.5% of 123457 DKK minor | `1852` (18.51855 rounds half up) |
| 1.5% of 99 DKK minor, three events | `1` (one rounding of 1.485, not three of 0.495) |
| 2.9% of 100000 + 25 × 180 | `7400` |
| 1% of 100000, minimum 5000 | `5000`, bound `minimum` |

## Upgrading: percentage prices

Nothing to do for existing prices. Code with a `match` over `PricingModel` that has no `default` arm should handle `Percentage`. Code that offers every `PricingModel::cases()` as a choice for a recurring price should leave `Percentage` out: it prices a reported value, not a quantity of seats or units.
